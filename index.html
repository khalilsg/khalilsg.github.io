Retirement Monte Carlo

Will a portfolio outlast the plan? Instead of assuming a flat average return, it resamples real market history — S&P 500, 10-year Treasuries, and CPI inflation together, 1928–2025 — across thousands of simulated lifetimes, and reports the probability the money lasts in today's dollars.

  • Full lifecycle — saving years and retirement years on one age-based timeline, with pensions, Social Security, fees, and taxes on withdrawals
  • Real strategies — fixed-real spending or Guyton-Klinger guardrails; a fixed stock/bond mix or a glide path
  • Find your levers — sensitivity sweeps, a two-parameter success heatmap, and a tornado chart ranking which assumption matters most
  • Sequence-of-returns risk — how much of the failure risk is the order of returns, and when you're past the danger zone
  • Shareable & private — scenarios encode into a link; private mode hides every dollar figure for screen sharing

Pokémon Damage Calculator

K Calc — a competitive Pokémon doubles toolsuite built for the Champions format. Paste a Showdown team, pick the opponent's six, and get every offensive and defensive matchup at once, recalculated live as the battle state changes.

  • Every matchup, not one — full offense and defense grids across ±6 stat stages, plus speed tiers and lead-pair scoring from Smogon usage data
  • Battle tracker — stat stages, weather, screens, Helping Hand, and KOs update every tab in real time
  • Team builder — slot-by-slot editor that exports a Showdown paste or loads straight into the calculator
  • Extra tools — OHKO thresholds, base-stat comparison, a movepool/ability finder, and match history with export
